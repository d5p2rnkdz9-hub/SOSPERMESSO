About Us

We are lawyers and jurists based in Turin. We work on the rights of foreign nationals in Italy. Many of us are members of ASGI (Association for Legal Studies on Immigration).

S.O.S. Permesso was conceived and created by Alberto Pasquero, lawyer and ASGI member, and was developed with the help of numerous people including:

  • Eleonora Celoria - Lawyer, ASGI member
  • Elena Garelli - Lawyer, ASGI member
  • Marylanda Abdullaj - Lawyer, ASGI member
  • Ilaria Ippolito, Jurist, ASGI member
  • Serena Olivetti, Jurist
  • Elena Tommasoni, Jurist
  • Germana Marchese, Jurist, ASGI member

🚀 Want to help out? We are always looking for people who want to contribute: translators, lawyers, designers, developers, or simply anyone who wants to help!
